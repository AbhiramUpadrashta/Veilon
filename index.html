How it works

Three moves. Zero clutter.

Veilon adds a separator | and an arrow › to your menu bar. Everything left of the separator can disappear on demand.

01
⌘ hold

⌘-drag to arrange

Hold Command and drag the icons you rarely use to the left of the | separator. macOS remembers the order.

02
⌥⌘B

Click to hide / show

Click the arrow — or press ⌥⌘B anywhere — and the hidden zone slides away. Click again to bring it back.

03

Auto-hide itself

Peeked at a hidden icon? Veilon tucks everything away again after 3 – 120 seconds. Set it once, forget it.

Under the hood

Why Hidden Bar broke on macOS 27 — and how Veilon fixes it

Menu bar hiders work by stretching an invisible separator until the icons to its left are pushed off-screen. macOS 26 quietly capped that width. macOS 27 deletes any menu bar item that reaches half the screen width — so the separator vanished and nothing got hidden.

Veilon keeps each piece just under the limit, adds invisible spacers for wide displays, and lets hidden icons flow into macOS 27's native « overflow — so they're still one click away.

✕ CLASSIC HIDERS ON macOS 27
Separator stretched to 10,000 pt → crosses the ½-screen line → removed by the system.
✓ BARVEIL ON macOS 27
Separator sized to just under ½ of your narrowest screen, plus spacers for big monitors.

Install guide

Up and running in a minute

Quit Hidden Bar, Vanilla, Ice or any other menu bar hider first — two hiders will fight each other.

Download

Click Download for Mac. You'll get the Veilon app in your Downloads folder.

Allow it to open

Double-click it. If macOS says it can't verify the app, click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway. Watch the video below.

Open & move

Double-click Veilon and click Move to Applications. It then lives in Applications and Launchpad.

Arrange & enjoy

Right-click › to start editing, ⌘-drag icons left of the | line, then right-click › again to lock it. Click › to show/hide.

First launch

macOS says it can't verify Veilon?

That's normal for free apps that aren't paid-notarized. Watch where to click — you only do this once.

Install without the security warning (recommended): open Terminal, paste this line and press Return. It downloads Veilon straight into your Applications folder and starts it — no “Apple could not verify” prompt. curl -fsSL https://abhiramupadrashta.github.io/Veilon/install.sh | bash

Downloaded from the browser instead? macOS shows a one-time warning because Veilon is a free indie app that isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway once — it won't ask again.

Build from source instead (needs Apple's free Command Line Tools): # one time: xcode-select --install git clone https://github.com/AbhiramUpadrashta/Veilon.git cd Hidden-Bar && bash build.sh --install

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Veilon really free?

Yes — Veilon is completely free. If it saves you some sanity, you can support development with a small donation.

Does it work on macOS 26 and older?

Yes. Veilon supports macOS 13 Ventura through macOS 27. It detects your version and uses the right hiding method automatically.

How do I update to a new version?

No need to come back here. Open Preferences → About → Check for Updates… (right-click › to edit, then right-click the line → Preferences). Veilon downloads the newest version, replaces itself and restarts. It also checks automatically about once a day. Installed Veilon before version 1.3? Older versions don't have this button yet — download Veilon once more from this page (your settings are kept). From then on, updates happen inside the app.

An icon disappeared — how do I get it back?

Click the arrow to expand. On macOS 27 you can also click the system « overflow button. Quitting Veilon always restores every icon.

Does it need Accessibility or Screen Recording permission?

No. Veilon uses only standard, public menu bar APIs. The global shortcut works without any special permissions.

Does it collect any data?

None. Veilon never connects to the internet.

How do I uninstall?

Right-click ›, right-click the line → Quit, and drag it from Applications to the Trash.
